# Direction

ChowderAI Lab focuses on building modern AI-native software while continuously improving engineering practices.

Our direction is intentionally short-term.

We continuously adapt based on experience, technology, and real-world feedback.

## Current Direction

- Build reusable engineering foundations.
- Create production-ready software.
- Develop AI engineering capabilities.
- Build reusable templates and blueprints.
- Contribute to open source.
- Share engineering knowledge through real projects.

Direction is reviewed continuously.

It changes when experience demonstrates a better path.
