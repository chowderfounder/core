# Repositories

Repositories organize software, templates, products, and organizational knowledge.

Each repository has a clear and focused purpose.

## Core

The organizational foundation.

Contains the organization's purpose, engineering standards, roadmap, and shared knowledge.

---

## Website

The public website for ChowderAI Lab.

Includes the landing page, journal, and public documentation.

---

## Templates

Reusable project templates.

Examples include backend, frontend, AI, and infrastructure templates.

---

## Products

Production software built to solve real-world problems.

Each product has its own repository.

---

## Shared

Reusable libraries, packages, SDKs, and internal tooling.

---

## Experiments

Short-lived prototypes and technology explorations.

Successful experiments may later become products, templates, or shared libraries.

## Repository Principles

- One clear purpose per repository.
- Keep repositories independent.
- Reuse before creating new repositories.
- Archive repositories that no longer provide value.
