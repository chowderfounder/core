# Operating Model

ChowderAI Lab is an AI-native organization.

It operates through a flat structure where humans and AI collaborate to build software, generate knowledge, and continuously improve engineering practices.

## Principles

- Keep the organization flat.
- Organize around capabilities, not hierarchy.
- AI augments engineering—it does not replace accountability.
- Automate repetitive work whenever practical.
- Small teams make decisions faster.

## Decision Making

Decisions should be made by the capability closest to the problem.

Significant engineering decisions should be documented.

## Continuous Evolution

The organization continuously adapts.

Processes, tools, and responsibilities evolve as better approaches emerge.
