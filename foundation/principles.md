# Principles

These principles guide every decision made within ChowderAI Lab.

Technologies evolve.

Frameworks change.

Principles endure.

---

## 1. Build Before Debating

The fastest way to learn is to build.

Ideas become knowledge only after they have been tested through real software.

---

## 2. Solve Real Problems

Every project should exist because it solves a meaningful problem.

Learning is a consequence of building useful software.

---

## 3. Simplicity Over Complexity

Prefer the simplest solution that solves the problem well.

Complexity should be introduced only when it is justified.

---

## 4. Evolution Over Prediction

Long-term plans are useful.

Continuous adaptation is essential.

We improve our direction through experience rather than trying to predict the future.

---

## 5. Engineering Before Technology

Good engineering outlasts any framework.

Architectures, patterns, and tools are selected because they solve problems—not because they are popular.

---

## 6. Learn Through Products

Products are our learning platform.

Every product should improve both the software and the organization.

---

## 7. Knowledge Must Be Reusable

Knowledge becomes valuable when it can be reused.

Reusable solutions become templates, blueprints, standards, or documentation.

---

## 8. Adopt Standards. Invent Only When Necessary.

Whenever a well-established standard solves the problem, adopt it.

Innovation should focus on solving new problems, not replacing existing standards without a clear reason.

---

## 9. Quality Is a Continuous Process

Quality is not a phase.

It is part of design, implementation, testing, deployment, operation, and continuous improvement.

---

## 10. Improve Every Iteration

Every commit, document, product, and decision should leave the organization in a better state than before.
