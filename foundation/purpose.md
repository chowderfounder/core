# Purpose

ChowderAI Lab exists to build AI-native software and continuously improve the way modern software is engineered.

We believe that the best way to learn is by solving real problems.

Every product we build has two goals:

- Solve a meaningful problem.
- Generate reusable engineering knowledge.

We do not optimize for learning technologies.

We optimize for building better software.

Technologies, frameworks, and architectures will evolve over time.

Our principles should not.

Knowledge becomes part of the organization only after it has been validated through real work.

We continuously adapt our practices based on experience, experimentation, and measurable outcomes.
